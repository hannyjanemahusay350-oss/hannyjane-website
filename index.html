My Reflection

Hello! I’m Hanny Jane Mahusay from Mati City, Davao Oriental. We used to live in Donluis Village, but we are now staying at Riverside Sudlon after our house was burned down.

I am a Grade 12 student at Mati National Comprehensive High School, taking ICT Programming. I have a unique way of speaking but people still understand me. I am shy and quiet, and I choose friends who have the same mindset and values as I do — not those who are too loud or always going out. I prefer simple friends who also have strict parents. I have three close friends, so we are four in all.

Sometimes I feel a little envious because they are all smart and achievers, but I never regret having them — they always help me, especially with our programming tasks. I admit I don’t understand codes easily and I find our strand difficult at times, but I am doing my best to learn. My goal is to take Information Technology in college. Some say it’s hard, but I believe no course is easy. I will just work hard and trust God to help me reach my dreams. I live a simple, peaceful, and happy life — and that means everything to me.
